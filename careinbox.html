How it works

Less time triaging. More time on care.

01

Connect your channels

Link your agency's email, WhatsApp Business number and Facebook Page. Messages from every channel arrive in one shared inbox.

02

AI sorts and drafts

Each message is sorted and summarised so the urgent ones stand out, and a suggested reply is drafted for your team.

03

Your team decides

A person at your agency reviews every message and chooses what to send. CareInbox never makes decisions about people on its own.

Data protection

Built for messages that mention health and care needs.

  • Your agency stays in control

    Your agency is the controller of its message data. We process it only on your documented instructions, under a data processing agreement.

  • No advertising or general AI training

    We never use message content or Meta platform data for advertising, profiling or to train general-purpose AI models. We do not sell personal data.

  • Secured by default

    Encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access, multi-factor authentication for staff access, access logging and regular backups.

  • Clear retention

    Message data is kept as you instruct, then deleted or returned within 30 days of your contract ending, unless the law requires us to keep it.

When you connect WhatsApp Business or a Facebook Page, we receive only the data Meta's platform makes available for the permissions you grant, and use it only to provide the connected features. Read the full details in our privacy policy.
