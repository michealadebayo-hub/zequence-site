1. Who we are

Zequence Digital UK Ltd (“Zequence”, “we”, “us”) is a company registered in England and Wales, company number 16384575. Our registered office is 41 Chapel Place, Seaton Burn, Newcastle upon Tyne, NE13 6EX, United Kingdom.

  • Email: hello@zequencedigital.co.uk
  • Website: zequencedigital.co.uk
  • ICO registration number: ZC096524

We act in two roles under UK data protection law:

  • Controller for data about our website visitors, enquirers, customers and their account users. We decide how and why that data is used.
  • Processor for data our customers put into or route through CareInbox, such as messages a home care agency receives from clients, families or staff. The customer (for example, the care agency) is the controller of that data, and we process it only on their documented instructions under a data processing agreement.

If your data reaches us through a CareInbox customer, that customer’s own privacy notice also applies, and they are your first point of contact.

4. AI processing, sharing and transfers

AI features. CareInbox uses AI models to sort, summarise and draft replies to incoming messages. A person at the care agency reviews and decides what to do. We do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about anyone by automated means alone.

Who we share data with. We share personal data only with service providers that help us run our services, under written contracts that require them to protect it:

  • Cloud hosting and database providers
  • AI model providers, used to process message content for CareInbox features
  • Email and messaging providers, including Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business, Messenger) when a customer connects those channels
  • Payment and accounting providers
  • Our professional advisers, such as accountants and lawyers

A current list of sub-processors is available on request from hello@zequencedigital.co.uk. We may also disclose data where the law requires it, or to protect rights and safety.

International transfers. Some providers may process data outside the UK. When they do, we use a UK adequacy decision or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement (or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses) to protect it.

We protect data with encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access controls, multi-factor authentication for staff access, logging of access to systems, and regular backups. Only people who need data to do their job can access it. If a breach affects your data, we will tell the affected customer without undue delay and notify the ICO where required.

6. Your rights, data deletion and contact

Your rights. You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or move it to another provider. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time. We reply within one month, and there is normally no charge.

How to delete your data (including data from Facebook or WhatsApp)

If you have used our services or interacted with us through a Meta product and want your data deleted:

  1. Email hello@zequencedigital.co.uk with the subject “Data deletion request”.
  2. Include your name and the email, phone number or Facebook/WhatsApp account you used with us.
  3. We will confirm your identity, delete your data within 30 days, and email you to confirm. We keep only what the law requires, such as invoices.

If your data came to us through a CareInbox customer (for example, a care agency you messaged), we will pass your request to them and act on their instructions.

Complaints. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy. We will post any changes on this page and update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will tell customers by email.
