# Finance Pack Benchmark

## Positioning

Most finance model template platforms compete on static Excel downloads, template breadth, or finance training. Zequence should position the finance pack as:

Interactive finance modelling workspace + downloadable formula workbook + variance interpretation + optional expert setup or custom finance pack.

## Offer Ladder

| Tier | Price | Purpose |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| DIY single template | £29-£79 | Low-friction entry product for self-serve users |
| Industry pack | £149-£299 | Bundle for one industry with guides, formulas, and dashboards |
| Expert review / audit | £250-£500 | Spreadsheet review, formula risk check, and improvement notes |
| Done-with-you setup | £750-£1,250 | Configure the pack with client data and assumptions |
| Custom Lite | From £1,500 | Tailored model mapped to the client chart of accounts and KPIs |
| Custom Pro | From £5,000 | Full forecast, variance, cash, scenario, dashboard, and board pack build |
| Monthly CFO pack | £750-£3,000/mo | Monthly refresh, commentary, and decision support |

## Competitor Signals

| Platform Type | Common Strength | Zequence Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Template marketplaces | Large catalogue and fast checkout | Match the catalogue, then add interactivity and interpretation |
| Low-cost template shops | Cheap entry templates | Use single templates as lead products, not the main margin |
| Training platforms | Credibility and learning assets | Add guides, walkthroughs, and model audit credibility |
| Custom finance consultants | High-value bespoke builds | Convert users from templates into setup and custom packs |

## Commercial Rule

Do not compete only as a spreadsheet shop. Use templates to create trust, then monetise the service layer: review, setup, custom build, and monthly reporting support.
